# Blocking

The basics to blocking in Dragon Ball FighterZ (DBFZ) is to block any attacks that come towards your character. To block you must be holding back. Holding back is pressing the direction that your character's back is facing. So if your character is looking to the right of the screen, you must be holding the left direction to block. If you're facing the left side of the screen you will hold the right direction to block. There are some things that cannot be blocked and only two ways to block in DBFZ, neutral, or low. These will be explained below. 
## Types of Blocking 

Blocking in DBFZ is easier than it sounds. T is to block either neutral (standing), for mid and high attacks, or to block low for any low or ground attacks. Blocking, however can not block any [[basics/throws|throws]]. Throws must be dodged by either a jumping, back dashing, or [[basics/vanish|vanishing]]. 
### When to block 

You will want to block anytime the opponent is throwing an attack that is coming towards and cannot be avoided. Its also beneficial to block to find spacing (timing that allows you to fight back) in the opponents attacks. Its better to block anytime that it is to throw out a late attack and immediately end up in a combo. You can also block enemy [[basics/super-dash|super dashes]].
## Tips for Beginners

- If the opponent is in the air coming towards you with a attack, immediately  stand and hold back so that you do not take any damage. 
- Blocking does not allow for you take any physical damage. [[basics/ki-blasts|Ki blasts]] and super attacks will deal chip damage on block, but will not kill you if your health is low.
- If you're ever unsure of what to do, its ALWAYS beneficial to hold back for surprise attacks from the enemy.
