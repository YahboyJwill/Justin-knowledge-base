# What is a Vanish?

A vanish is a move typically performed by pressing M+H together. It costs one bar of meter ,but this move allows you to teleport behind the opponent to either 
1. Teleport out of a bad situtaion
2. continue a combo for a little extra damage

## Why should I vanish.

You should incorporate vanishes into your basics fighting so that you have more option for whatever situation you end up calls for. Vanishing after a combo will allow for you to continue your combo to squeeze some extra damage out. Every confirmed hit counts so vanishing is a great way to turn the tides in your favor 

### How do i use a vanish defensively? 

Using a vanish defensively is great because if your opponent is hitting you with a barrage of attacks on your [[basics/blocking|block]], you can use your vanish in between attacks to get out and kick your opponent away. Another use is to use it immediately when your opponent throws out a [[basics/super-dash|super dash]], [[basics/throws|throw]] or [[basics/ki-blasts|ki blasts]] to get behind them and create some space.
