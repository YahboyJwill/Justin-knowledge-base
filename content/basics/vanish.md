# What is a Vanish?

A vanish is a move typically performed by pressing M+H together. This move allows you to teleport behind the opponent to either 
1. Teleport out of a bad situtaion
2. continue a combo for a little extra damage
These are the basics to using a vanish. 

## Why should I vanish.

You should incorporate vanishes into your basics fighting so that you have more option for whatever situation you end up calls for. Vanishing after a combo will allow for you to continue your combo to squeeze some extra damage out. Every confirmed hit counts so vanishing is a great way to turn the tides in your favor 

## How do i use a vanish defensively? 

Using a vanish defensively is great because if your opponent is hitting you with a barrage of attacks on your block, you can use your teleport in between attacks to get out and kick your opponent away. It can also be used if you use it immediately when your opponent throws out a super attack.
