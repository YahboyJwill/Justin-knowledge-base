# Throws

Throws or command grabs are a high risk special move that allow for a character to grab a [[basics/blocking|blocking]], or unsuspecting opponent and deal big damage. They deal big damage because of how risky it is to perform it so its always a game of risk versus reward. Currently in the game there are two different types of grabs. Dragon Rushes, Command Grabs.

## Dragon rush

Dragon rushes are a type of grab that can be performed by the entire roster in DBFZ. They are considered the least risky to perform since they start the beginning of a combo for the roster but should still only be used to keep your enemy guessing. 

## Command Grabs

Command grabs are a higher risk because they have a long framerate recovery if you miss the attack. Command grabs do big damage if successfully confirmed but require precise directional control in order to perform. Not all characters have a command grab so its worth noting who can perform them so you don't get caught off guard. 

### How do I counter a grab?

Countering a grab depends on which one is thrown at you. If its a Dragon rush, you just need to press a attack button BEFORE the dragon rush hits you. If done correctly you will end up in a clash that will push both fighters back and allow you to regain some distance. You can also [[basics/super-dash|super dash]] out, or throw some [[basics/ki-blasts|ki blasts]] to get some damage on the opponent too. 

Countering a command grab is a little different. You can also press a button just before it connects, but this puts you on the offensive which is great because it could lead to big damage on your opponent. Some command grabs though have armor, so its best to try and jump away from the opponent or [[basics/vanish|vanish]] to ensure you get away from potential danger.
