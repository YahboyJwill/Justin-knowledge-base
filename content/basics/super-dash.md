# Super dashing 

Super dashing is a fundamental move that allows for a character to do a super high speed dash (or jump if your Master Roshi) and close the gap between you and your opponent. Super dash is great against characters that are throwing low attacks, or shooting key blasts. All characters have a super dash tand must be used wisely or else you will be countered hard. 

## How do I counter a super dash?

You can counter a super dash with any basic attack or super attack. [[basics/ki-blasts|Ki blasts]] are the only thing that you do not want to use against a enemy super dash. Because of the vulnerable state that your character is in while you are super dashing, any medium or high heighted physical or any upgraded attack will punish your move. 

### When do I super dash? 

You should super dash from the following circumstances
1. The enemy is shooting ki blasts at you.
2. The enemy overcommitted a low attack.
3. Extending a combo
